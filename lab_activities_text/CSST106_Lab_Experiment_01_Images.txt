 Laboratory Activity: Introduction to Computer Vision
Objective:
To familiarize students with the concept of computer vision, understand how digital images are represented and manipulated, and perform basic image operations using OpenCV.
Materials Needed:
Computer with Python and OpenCV library installed
Sample images (color and grayscale)

Activity Steps:
Part 1: Understanding Digital Images
Introduction (Brief Lecture)
Explain what an image is in computer vision (a 2D/3D array of pixel values).
Discuss color spaces: grayscale vs. color images.
Introduce image representation (pixel intensity, channels, resolution).
Hands-on Exercise: Load and Explore an Image
Load a color image using OpenCV (cv2.imread).
Display the image (cv2.imshow) and print its shape (img.shape).
Identify the dimensions (height, width, channels) and explain the BGR color ordering.
Convert the image to grayscale and display the grayscale image.
Explore Image Properties
Examine pixel values at specific coordinates.
Change a pixel’s color to a new value and observe the change.

Part 2: Basic Image Operations
Image Manipulation
Crop a region of interest (ROI) from the image.
Resize the image to a smaller or larger dimension.
Rotate the image by 90 degrees.
Summary Discussion
How images are digitally represented and manipulated.
The significance of color channels and pixel values.
Real-life applications of computer vision involving image processing.

Activities to be passed on a Hardcopy:
Make a short report describing the steps performed, what was learned about image representation and manipulation.
Screenshots of original, grayscale, and manipulated images.

This introduction activity provides a hands-on understanding of fundamental concepts in computer vision and prepares students for more advanced topics like feature extraction and object detection.
